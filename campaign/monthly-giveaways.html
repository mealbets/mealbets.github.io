The details

Monthly Giveaway — Official Rules

Please read the rules below before taking part.

  1. Schedule

    Beginning in December 2026, MealBets runs an ongoing monthly giveaway, with a separate draw for each month. Draws take place within five business days after the month ends. Each month’s start date and prize details are announced on the relevant monthly activity page.

  2. Eligibility

    The giveaway is for eligible MealBets users who are active during that month. MealBets employees, people directly involved in administering the draw, and members of their households are not eligible.

  3. Automatic entry

    Eligible active users are entered automatically in that month’s draw. No registration form, invitation acceptance, or email confirmation is required.

  4. Entries and fairness

    Three users are randomly selected each month. MealBets may verify and remove duplicate records if the same delivery user uses multiple accounts or repeated entries to gain additional chances.

  5. Prizes

    Three gifts are awarded to three winning households each month. Prizes are mainly brand-new, practical household and lifestyle items. Monthly prizes are randomly chosen from a separate prize list. Published prize details include the item name, a basic description, estimated retail value in CAD, and any applicable conditions or restrictions.

    Prizes cannot be exchanged for cash. If an advertised prize is unavailable because of supply issues, MealBets may substitute a prize of equal or greater value.

  6. Draw process

    After entries close each month, MealBets randomly selects three potential winners. Every valid participant has an equal chance, and each household may receive no more than one prize per month. The draw is recorded in full, and a privacy-protected draw record is shared on official social media.

  7. Notification and confirmation

    Potential winners are notified at their MealBets account email address. They must reply within five business days of the notice, confirm their residence and eligibility, and provide the contact details needed to receive the prize. If a potential winner does not reply on time or complete verification, MealBets may randomly select a replacement from the remaining valid participants.

  8. Prize collection and delivery

    After eligibility is confirmed, MealBets contacts each winning household to arrange collection or delivery, aiming to provide the prize within 30 days of confirmation. Winners pay no prize-claim fee.

    Manufacturer instructions, product-use risks, after-sales service, and warranties are governed by the applicable product terms and laws. MealBets accepts no legal liability for the prize products, subject to applicable law.

  9. Privacy

    Personal information collected for the giveaway is used only to manage entries, verify eligibility, conduct the draw, notify winners, deliver prizes, and maintain necessary activity records. Public draw videos use participant numbers or other privacy safeguards. Participants’ full names, email addresses, home addresses, and children’s information are not disclosed.

  10. Announcements and changes

    Monthly prizes, start dates, draw dates, and related announcements are published through the MealBets website, app, or official social media. MealBets may make reasonable changes to future giveaway arrangements.

    Organizer: MealBets. Questions may be directed to MealBets customer service.
