MealBets account credit

Top up by e-Transfer.
Instant credit when funds arrive.

Add funds to your MealBets account with Interac e-Transfer. Once we receive your funds, your account credit is loaded automatically and a confirmation is sent to your MealBets account email.

Your transfer details

Required — do not skip

Transfer message

Only your registered phone number

Use the phone number registered to your MealBets account. Do not add a name, greeting, or other text to the transfer message.

Without the correct phone number, we cannot automatically match your transfer to your account.

Send your e-Transfer to emt@deposit.mealbets.com

Three simple steps

  1. Set up your e-Transfer

    In your banking app or online banking, enter the recipient email shown here and choose your top-up amount.

  2. Add your phone number to the message

    In the transfer message, enter only the phone number registered to your MealBets account so we can match the funds to your account.

  3. Review and send

    Check the recipient email, amount, and phone number in the transfer message, then send your e-Transfer.

Funds received. Credit added. Email sent.

Your top-up is processed automatically once the funds are received. Bank processing times can vary; sending a transfer does not mean the funds have arrived yet.

MealBets charges no processing fee for e-Transfer top-ups.

Your bank may charge an e-Transfer fee depending on your bank and account type. Check with your bank for details.

Need help with a transfer?

If you entered the wrong phone number, forgot the transfer message, or your funds have arrived but your credit is missing, contact our team for help.
