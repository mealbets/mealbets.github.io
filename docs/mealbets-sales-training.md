# MealBets: Features & Advantages

Internal sales training for conversations with parents, school administrators, and school councils. Prepared September 23, 2026, from the website repository and the supplied school outreach material.

**Lunch Means More.** MealBets combines app-based ordering, freshly prepared meals, and organized school delivery to make lunch easier for families and schools.

## Sales Points for Schools

Lead with reduced administration, dependable delivery arrangements, documented practices, and school-community benefits.

### Administration & Oversight

- **Family-managed ordering and payment:** Parents make selections and pay through the platform, reducing manual order collection and payment administration for schools.
- **Automated order communication:** The system handles order changes and SMS/email notifications, reducing repetitive coordination work.
- **Prepared meal labels:** Orders arrive identified for school distribution, simplifying the matching process at lunchtime.
- **Dedicated school portal:** Administrators can monitor order and delivery information, providing oversight without managing every family transaction.
- **Agreed delivery arrangements:** MealBets works with each school to establish available dates and the drop-off process before service begins.
- **Flexible logistics discussions:** The supplied outreach material offers accommodation of school-specific operational needs where possible; agree on the details during onboarding.
- **Reviewable supplier documentation:** Schools can request or review food-safety, menu-review, and insurance materials as part of their vendor assessment.

### Delivery & Operational Support

- **Grouped deliveries by an in-house team:** Background-checked drivers bring orders to the agreed school location, providing a coordinated handoff.
- **Lunch-period coordination:** Delivery dates and drop-off arrangements are agreed with the school to fit its lunch routine.
- **Student-identifying labels:** Grade, teacher, and student names help school staff match meals to students; younger children may still need pickup assistance.
- **Automated family notifications:** SMS delivery updates and system communications reduce routine requests for school staff to confirm arrival.
- **Backup meals and lunchtime support:** Backup meals and a rapid-response contact give schools an operational contact when an order cannot be located or an unexpected issue arises.
- **Direct parent support:** MealBets handles ordering questions and user support, helping keep routine inquiries away from the school office.

### Nutrition, Safety & Vendor Review

- **Dietitian involvement:** Meals are developed with nutrition expertise, supporting a school discussion focused on children's nutritional needs.
- **PPM-150-aligned categories:** All categories except Deluxe Full Meal are described as PPM-150 compliant; retain that exception when discussing eligible menus.
- **Licensed commercial kitchens:** Freshly prepared meals come from licensed kitchen operations, with published inspection and food-handler documentation available for review.
- **Stated allergen exclusions:** Meals are described as peanut-, tree-nut-, and shellfish-free. Other allergens may be present, and batch-prepared meals cannot be customized.
- **Documented handling controls:** The site describes a no-bare-hands policy, KN-95 respirators during post-heating packaging, and food sample retention for more than 10 days.
- **Published insurance coverage:** The homepage advertises $5 million in business insurance; verify the current policy and coverage terms during vendor review.

### Benefits for the School Community

- **Broad menu choice:** Six meal categories and more than 140 overall options offer families variety across different tastes and appetites; actual availability depends on the school's menu.
- **Parent-controlled portions and spending:** Families choose meals and available portion sizes, with prices starting at $7.99 CAD and totals displayed before payment.
- **No school-lunch service or delivery fees:** Included grouped delivery makes program costs easier for families to understand.
- **Accessible program information:** English and Simplified Chinese website content helps schools share information with more families.

### Partnerships & Fundraising

- **School fundraising options:** The FAQ describes flexible fundraising arrangements that can be discussed with administrators and school councils.
- **Proposed 5% revenue contribution:** The supplied outreach offer provides a 5% contribution for schools promoting the program through existing channels such as email. Confirm the revenue basis, eligible orders, payment schedule, and agreed terms before presenting it as a commitment.
- **Use of existing communication channels:** School newsletters and emails can introduce the service to families without requiring a separate ordering campaign infrastructure.
- **Vendor-channel listings:** The supplied material states that MealBets is listed on LunchBox and APP for YRDSB/TDSB. Confirm the exact platform names, current listing, and approval scope before quoting this to a school.
- **Parent-demand-led introductions:** Interest from families can open a conversation with school administration or council. Hillmount Public School is the example supplied, not evidence of a completed partnership.

### Experience & Service Coverage

- **50+ participating schools:** Current site copy reports service to more than 50 schools, providing an indicator of operational experience.
- **School service since May 2024:** The FAQ and supplied outreach material identify this as the start of school service.
- **Five named service areas:** Toronto, North York, Scarborough, Markham, and Richmond Hill are the stated service areas; confirm availability for the individual school.
- **New-school onboarding discussions:** A school name, location, and contact person provide a starting point for assessing service and delivery arrangements.
- **Camp and community experience:** The site also describes camp and community pickup services, relevant when discussing other programs in a school community.

### Suggested Conversation

- **Administrator opening:** “Families handle ordering and payment, while MealBets prepares labeled meals and coordinates delivery. Your school can monitor orders and deliveries through a dedicated portal.”
- **School council opening:** “The program gives families more lunch options and can support school fundraising. We can discuss a contribution arrangement and how to introduce the service through your existing communications.”
- **Next step:** Arrange a discussion covering delivery dates, pickup responsibilities, portal access, vendor requirements, and fundraising terms.

## Sales Points for Parents

Lead with less lunch preparation, meals children can choose, clear pricing, and an organized school pickup experience.

### Convenience & Time Savings

- **Less daily lunch preparation:** Parents can replace morning cooking and packing with advance ordering, saving time on busy school days.
- **Mobile ordering:** The MealBets app is available for iPhone and Android, allowing families to browse menus and manage orders from their phones.
- **Student-specific setup:** Parents select a school and create a Meal Note for each student, helping match meals to the right child.
- **Choice of dates, meals, and portions:** Families choose from the options available for their school rather than relying on a single fixed meal.
- **Advance menu visibility:** The app shows the current one-to-four-week menu, helping parents plan ahead and involve children in choosing lunch.
- **Clear order review:** Parents can check My Orders after checkout to confirm their selections and student details.
- **Self-service cancellation:** Upcoming orders can be canceled in the app before the published cutoff, giving families a way to respond to changing plans.

### Variety & Meals Children Enjoy

- **More than 140 meal options:** A broad overall menu gives families alternatives to repetitive packed lunches; availability varies by school and date.
- **Six meal categories:** Asian Cuisine, World's Flavors, Prime Sushi, Deluxe Full Meal, Burger & Sandwich, and Wraps Delight offer different formats and flavors.
- **Familiar favorites and different cuisines:** Options such as pasta, rice meals, sushi, burgers, and wraps help families find meals that appeal to their children.
- **Continued menu development:** New dishes and regular updates help keep the lunch experience interesting.
- **Feedback-informed menus:** The site describes menu updates based on customer feedback, seasonal ingredients, and nutritional balance.
- **Meal photos and descriptions:** Families can preview meals and review ingredients and dietary information before choosing.
- **Vegetarian examples:** The sample menu includes vegetable sushi and a chickpea wrap, providing additional choices when available in the app.

### Nutrition & Portion Choice

- **Dietitian involvement:** Meals are developed with nutrition expertise; the website identifies consulting dietitian Yijing Catherine Cai as an Ontario registered dietitian.
- **Focus on growing children:** Menus are designed with children's nutritional needs in mind, helping parents consider balance alongside taste.
- **PPM-150-aligned categories:** All categories except Deluxe Full Meal are described as PPM-150 compliant, supporting school nutrition requirements within that scope.
- **Three typical portion sizes:** Asian Cuisine and World's Flavors offer approximately 260 g, 350 g, and 450 g portions, helping parents choose for different appetites.
- **Age-based starting guidance:** XS is suggested for JK–SK, Small for Grades 1–3, and Large for Grades 4–8 and above; individual appetites vary.
- **Consistent portioning:** The FAQ describes standard preparation procedures and proportional protein, vegetable, and carbohydrate quantities across those sizes.
- **Clear sushi quantities:** Sushi portions are shown by roll or piece count, making the quantity easier to understand before ordering.

### Fresh Preparation & Food Information

- **Fresh daily preparation:** Meals are freshly cooked in licensed commercial kitchens, offering families a prepared-lunch alternative to packing food at home.
- **Peanut-, tree-nut-, and shellfish-free meals:** MealBets states that meals exclude these ingredients, addressing common school and family concerns within its stated allergen policy.
- **Ingredient visibility:** Main ingredients and dietary restrictions are listed in the app so parents can make informed selections.
- **Support with ingredient questions:** Families can contact MealBets to clarify meal information before ordering.
- **Documented handling controls:** The homepage describes a no-bare-hands policy and mandatory KN-95 respirators during post-heating packaging.
- **Food sample retention:** The site states that food samples are retained for more than 10 days, supporting operational follow-up when concerns arise.
- **Qualifications available for review:** Published materials include a DineSafe inspection certificate, food-handler certificate, and dietitian menu-review/PPM-150 confirmation documentation.

### Delivery, Identification & Pickup

- **Grouped school delivery:** Orders travel together to an agreed school lunch drop-off point, bringing lunch directly to the school pickup location.
- **In-house delivery team:** MealBets uses its own drivers; the flyer and supplied outreach material describe them as background-checked.
- **Lunch-period coordination:** Delivery arrangements are planned around school lunch timing, helping families and schools organize pickup.
- **Student-identifying labels:** Meal Notes include grade, teacher's name, and the child's full name, helping staff and students identify each order.
- **Automated SMS notifications:** Delivery or pickup messages keep families informed without requiring them to contact the school for routine updates.
- **Elementary-school pickup guidance:** Meals usually go to the designated lunch table; younger children may receive school staff assistance in finding their order.
- **High-school pickup guidance:** Students receive pickup instructions for the MealBets vehicle, which carries a visible roof banner; the app also provides a school pickup-location photo.
- **Utensils included:** Free utensils are attached or provided with meals, reducing another item families need to remember.

### Support When Families Need It

- **Backup meals:** The FAQ states that drivers carry backup meals to help address unexpected situations at lunchtime.
- **Lunchtime rapid-response support:** If a meal cannot be located, parents or schools can call MealBets for priority assistance locating it or determining the next step.
- **Multiple contact channels:** Phone, email, WeChat, and in-app support give families several ways to ask questions or resolve issues.

### Pricing & Family Value

- **Meals starting at $7.99 CAD:** Entry-level options give families a clear starting point; actual prices depend on the meal and portion shown in the app.
- **No school-lunch service fee:** Participating-school orders have no separate MealBets service fee, making costs easier to understand.
- **No school-lunch delivery fee:** Grouped delivery to the designated school location is included.
- **Visible total before payment:** Pricing updates with meal and size selections, allowing parents to review the amount before checkout.
- **School-meal tax treatment stated in the FAQ:** The site says no HST is charged on public, Catholic, and private school meals; camps and other locations may have applicable taxes. Confirm the order total in the app.
- **Major cards accepted:** Visa, Mastercard, and American Express support familiar payment methods, with charges in Canadian dollars.

### Availability & Accessibility

- **50+ participating schools:** Current site copy reports more than 50 schools served, giving families a useful indicator of operating experience.
- **Service since May 2024:** The FAQ and supplied outreach material place the start of school service in May 2024.
- **Five named service areas:** The site identifies Toronto, North York, Scarborough, Markham, and Richmond Hill; actual school availability is checked in the app.
- **Requests for new schools:** Families and school representatives can submit a school name, location, and contact person for an availability discussion.
- **Service beyond schools:** MealBets also describes camp and community pickup services, offering potential continuity for families outside regular school days where available.
- **English and Simplified Chinese website:** The shared language switcher makes program information accessible to more families. This is a website feature, not a claim about app or support language coverage.

### Suggested Conversation

- **Parent opening:** “Choose your child's meal and portion in the app, and we'll deliver it to their participating school. There are no school-lunch service or delivery fees.”
- **When asked about food suitability:** Explain the stated allergen exclusions and ingredient information, then direct meal-specific questions to MealBets support.
- **Next step:** Help the parent check school availability, create an accurate Meal Note, and review available meals, portions, and delivery dates in the app.

## Shared Sales Guidance

- **PPM-150 has an exception:** Always retain the Deluxe Full Meal exclusion. The short flyer badges are broader than the detailed food-safety and school-program pages.
- **Reliable delivery is not a 100% guarantee:** The supplied email says “100% on-time,” but the FAQ acknowledges traffic, weather, and operational delays. Describe planned timing and support rather than promising perfect performance.
- **Minimal administration is not zero staff involvement:** MealBets handles ordering and delivery, but schools may still coordinate distribution and help JK/SK students retrieve meals.
- **Allergen exclusions are not universal allergy suitability:** Other allergens may be present. Meals are batch-prepared, custom ingredient changes are unavailable, and families should review details and contact support before ordering.
- **Availability is school-specific:** Do not imply that every school, every weekday, or all 140+ dishes are available to every family. Use the school's dates and menu in the app.
- **Use the newer school count:** The supplied outreach says 40+ schools; current site copy says 50+. Reconfirm the figure before future campaigns, and do not describe service as Ontario-wide.
- **Confirm vendor and fundraising terms:** Treat the platform listings and 5% contribution as supplied partnership information requiring confirmation for the specific school, not a universal agreement or board endorsement.
- **Quote credentials precisely:** The site uses dietitian and nutritionist wording in different places. Use the published consulting dietitian's title and current documentation rather than inventing certifications.
- **Respect the order cutoff:** The site states a 2 PM cutoff on the day before delivery for ordering/cancellation. Do not promise changes after the cutoff; confirm date-specific rules in the app.
- **Keep promotions separate from lasting advantages:** The site currently lists code MBBACK26 for a $7.99 new-user credit through September 30, 2026. It is not a permanent offer or an unconditional free meal of any price.
