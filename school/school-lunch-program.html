How It Works

A Clear Delivery Process

  1. Confirm service details. MealBets works with the school to establish the lunch drop-off process and available delivery dates.
  2. Families order in the app. Parents select their school, student meal note, delivery date, meal, and portion.
  3. Meals are prepared and labeled. Orders are organized for efficient school distribution.
  4. MealBets delivers. In-house drivers deliver the grouped orders to the designated school location.

Food quality and safety

  • Meals are prepared through our licensed commercial kitchen operations.
  • Menus are reviewed with nutrition expertise.
  • Meals are free from peanuts, tree nuts, and shellfish.
  • All categories except Deluxe Full Meal are PPM-150 compliant.
