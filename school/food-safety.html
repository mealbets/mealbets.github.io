School Lunch Food Safety and Nutrition

MealBets combines documented kitchen and delivery controls with clear meal information so families and schools can make informed lunch choices.

Our Practices

What Families Should Know

Allergen practices

MealBets meals are free from peanuts, tree nuts, and shellfish. Other allergens may be present, and meals cannot be customized because they are prepared in batches.

Review each meal’s ingredients and dietary information in the app before ordering. Contact us when you need help confirming ingredient information.

Nutrition standards

All MealBets categories except Deluxe Full Meal are designed to comply with Ontario Policy/Program Memorandum 150 nutrition standards.

Read Ontario’s official PPM 150 policy.

Kitchen controls

Meals are prepared through licensed commercial kitchen operations. MealBets publishes its current inspection, food-handler, insurance, and menu-review documents for families and schools to review.

Labeled school delivery

Orders are labeled using the student meal note supplied by the parent and delivered in groups to the school’s designated lunch drop-off point.
