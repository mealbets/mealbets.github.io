Delivery Locations

School Lunch Delivery Across Toronto and the GTA

MealBets delivers grouped lunch orders to 40+ participating schools. Current school availability appears in the MealBets app.

Check Your School in the App

Current Coverage

Five Service Areas

Toronto

Lunch delivery is available at participating schools within the City of Toronto.

North York

Families at participating North York schools can select their school and delivery dates in the app.

Scarborough

MealBets serves participating school communities in Scarborough.

Markham

Participating Markham schools receive grouped orders at a designated lunch drop-off location.

Richmond Hill

Lunch ordering and school delivery are available at participating Richmond Hill schools.

How to Check Your School

  1. Download or open the MealBets app.
  2. Create a student meal note and open the school selector.
  3. Choose your school to see available delivery dates and meals.

For student privacy and school-board policy compliance, individual participating-school names are not published on this public website.
